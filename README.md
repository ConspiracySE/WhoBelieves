# Who Believes?

A classroom ranking game for the seminar "Understanding Conspiracy Theories: Actors & Audiences" (PLUS Salzburg).

- Students: open the page and place the seven fictional people on the line.
- Teacher: the codes for steps 2 and 3 and the discussion are on the Week 8 slides.

The characters are fictional. Research rankings describe averages across many people, not predictions about individuals.
